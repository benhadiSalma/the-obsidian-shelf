Rules of the Archive

Every reader entering The Obsidian Shelf agrees to respect the collection, the curators, and the quiet dignity of the sanctuary.

Rule I — Live Stock & ISBN Registry

Every Tome Carries a Unique Seal

Each manuscript is cataloged with a unique ISBN identifier, live stock counter, and curator pricing. Reserving a book immediately deducts it from the public vault.

Rule II — Pickup & Counter Payment

Honor Your Reservations
